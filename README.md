## 💡 Project Summary
Built an end-to-end Power BI dashboard on **1M+ e-commerce transactions** to analyze sales performance, discount strategies, and return behavior, delivering actionable insights for **revenue optimization and business decision-making**.

## 🚀 Overview
This project analyzes how **discount ranges influence sales, revenue, returns, and customer behavior** to identify optimal pricing strategies and operational improvements.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics
- 💰 Total Revenue: 8.82 Billion  
- 🛒 Total Orders: 1M+  
- 👥 Total Users: 603K+  
- 🔁 Return Rate: 11.6%  
- ✅ Success Rate: 88.4%  

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights
- 📊 Peak sales occur in **20–30% discount range**  
- 💰 Maximum revenue at **10–20% discount (~2.9bn)**  
- ⚠️ Discounts above **40% reduce revenue efficiency**  
- 🔄 Return rate remains stable (~11–12%)  
- 🛒 Electronics category drives highest order volume  
- ⭐ High revenue products are rated between **4–4.5**  
- 🚚 ~30% of orders delayed/in transit  

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions Answered
- Which discount range maximizes revenue and orders?  
- Do higher discounts reduce return rates?  
- Which category drives the most sales?  
- How does pricing influence customer behavior?  
- Are there operational inefficiencies in delivery?  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- 📌 KPI cards for business overview

- 🏆 Top product performance tracking  
- 🔁 Orders vs Returns comparison  
- ⭐ Pricing vs Rating analysis  
- 🚚 Delivery status breakdown  
- ⏳ Time intelligence (MTD, QTD, YTD)  
- 🎛️ Interactive slicers (Category, Subcategory, Quarter, Month, Location)
